## The five patterns

1. **Deals are signed AT events.** Almosafer announced its Ministry of Hajj partnership and
   Tasheer deal at the Hajj Expo; its Saudia deal was signed at ATM Dubai; Kanoo launched its
   Desert Adventures JV at ATM; UZF 2026 produced 5,400+ signed agreements in three days.
   → Arrive with an agreement drafted; use the event as the stage to close and announce it.
2. **Awards are a sales channel — Direct already wins them.** Direct = WTA Saudi Arabia's
   Leading Visa Services Agency **2023, 2024 and 2025** + Arabian Best in Business Award.
   Almosafer Business holds WTA Leading Travel Management Company 2025 (the category to enter
   next); dnata took the corporate prize at the new Arabian Travel Awards Saudi 2025; Elaf
   enters every year.
3. **Booths are a rich-brand game, played selectively.** Almatar's first-ever booth is at WTM
   Spotlight Riyadh (Sep 2026 — we'll be on that floor); Almosafer is a WTM London premier
   partner; Wego sponsors Intermobility. Even they pick 1–2 stages/year → our 3-stand plan is right.
4. **Corporate-travel rivals are quiet operators** (Flyakeed = PIF-backed product play; elaa =
   Seera's corporate/government arm; Fursan = Amex GBT badge; dnata = awards + implants).
   Nobody owns the business-travel conversation at Saudi events — empty seat for Direct at
   Saudi Event Show / International MICE Summit / GBTA ME forum (Dubai, Feb).
5. **Exhibitor lists are public — mining confirmed.** LEAP and Money20/20 publish exhibitor
   directories (links now stored on those events in the hub); UZF had 150+ exhibitors,
   Intermobility 100+. Every exhibitor at ANY event is by definition a business-travel
   spender — the event doesn't need to be a travel event.
