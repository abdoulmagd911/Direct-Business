# v2 — future modules (later, not v1)

The modules that come after v1 and the pilot (V70, V517). The full scout specs for modules 1–9 live in the Drive
knowledge base, "21 Future modules" (the same text as the Project doc `claude/future-modules.md`); this list keeps
their numbers so every document uses the same ones. Nothing here is built, planned into a step, or switched on until
the owner says so. Every module inherits v2's rules (one organisation record with its sides, live names, every change
logged and undoable, Happened on decides the period, Riyadh time, admins-only Settings, the banned words, no real
data in the repository) and never duplicates Direct's own systems (V515).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Report generator | Designed monthly, quarterly and annual reports, the per-client business-review pack, a leadership deck — on the P6 report engine | 2 | S–M |
| 2 | Airlines and providers analytics | Which airlines and providers carry the volume, each deal's pace, the incentives earned and received | 9 | M–L |
| 3 | Supplier evaluation | A repeatable supplier scorecard and a recorded keep, improve or phase-out decision | 8 | S–M |

| 7 | Guarantees | Every guarantee received or given, with expiry reminders | 4 | S |
