# Direct — Company Identity System

Assembled 2026-08-12 from every real source we have: the official profile PDFs, the
standalone logo files, the brand kit on Google Drive, the three live Direct systems, this
app, the events page, and the designs earlier Claude sessions produced. Nothing here was
invented — every color was sampled from a real Direct file, every rule comes from something
already in use.

**The one-sentence summary:** Direct has one brand (orange, bilingual, "one accountable
partner") expressed through **three identities**, one per kind of output. Pick the identity
by what you're making, never by taste.

## The tender anatomy — from the real HRC technical offer (added 2026-08-12)

`techincal offer final 1.pdf` (Direct → Human Rights Commission) is the definitive
structure for tender responses, in Arabic:

**Technical offer (العرض الفني):** about Direct → work plan in 4 phases with durations
(خطة العمل) → scope: numbered services, each with its "technical process" bullets
(نطاق العمل + العملية التقنية) → timeline (الجدول الزمني) → services → **past projects
with client, scope and contract value** (سابقة الأعمال: Ma'aden 2M ·
Al Hilal 1.5M · Al Riyadh Club 1M · SFDA 500K) → named team with degrees (فريق العمل) →
**quantities table WITHOUT prices** (جدول الكميات بدون أسعار) → official certificates
(المستندات الرسمية: Monsha'at SME cert, CR 1010611219, etc.).

**Financial offer (العرض المالي), separate:** the same quantities table WITH prices
(number + in words), monthly payment schedule (جدول الدفعات), totals: subtotal → VAT →
total incl. VAT, each in numbers and words → official documents.

Fee model in tenders: كل دفعة = تكلفة الخدمة المطلوبة + رسوم الخدمة التعاقدية (cost of the
requested service + the contracted service fee) — matching the house fee model. This is
the blueprint for a future "tender mode" in the Proposal Studio.

## The three oranges — this is not a mistake

Three orange values exist in real Direct material. Each has a job:

| Orange | Value | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Document orange** | `#F06820` | Proposals, profiles, offers, agreements — anything printed or PDF'd. Slightly muted, print-safe. |
| **Logo-mark orange** | `#FF6C00` | The logo itself, favicons, app icons, tiny marks — anywhere the actual logo artwork appears small. |
| **Product orange** | `#F47A1F` | This app's UI. Sits between the other two; consistent with the hub (`#fc8004`) and corporate portal (`#e66000`) family. |

Never "fix" one to match another. They are siblings, not typos.

## Identity A — Direct Classic · الرسمية

**For:** everything a client, partner, or government body sees. Company profile, proposals
(technical/financial), service-fee offers, cooperation agreements, letters, certificates,
flyers.

- **Colors:** orange `#F06820`, table header `#F87020`, cover/closing gradient
  `#E54525 → #F26721`, gold accent `#FBAE16`, ink `#303848`, muted `#6B7480`,
  hairline `#E6E8EC`, wash `#F6F7F9`, wash-orange `#FFF3EC`, white.
- **Type:** EN Proxima Nova (Alt) 400/600/700 · AR 29LT Zarid Slab 400–900 ·
  Bahij TheSansArabic for price-table emphasis. Arabic always RTL.
- **Shapes:** rounded cards (12–16px) with soft shadows, orange stat numbers in white icon
  tiles, orange section titles top-left, logo top-center, swoosh footer +
  `www.directksa.com`, orange page-number tab on the right edge, solid-orange closing page.
- **Signature element:** the service-fee table — `#F87020` header, white bold text, columns
  `# | Service | الخدمة | Fee`, zebra rows, fees in orange bold, T&C note beneath.
- **Voice:** "Global supplier power. Saudi service. One partner." /
  «قوة موردين عالمية. خدمة سعودية. شريك واحد.»

## Identity B — Direct Editorial · التحريرية

**For:** internal reading — board readouts, working proposals, analyses, reports, plans.
Anything meant to be *read and decided on* rather than presented.

- **Colors (light):** paper `#FAF8F4`, surface white, panel `#F4F0E9`, ink `#2B2620`,
  muted `#7C7468`, line `#E9E3D9`, accent `#EC6620`, deep accent `#D9431F`, gold `#C9820C`,
  counterpoint teal `#3F6675` (for "theirs/Direct's side" vs our orange), good `#2E9E6B`.
- **Colors (dark):** ground `#15120E`, surface `#1E1A15`, ink `#F1ECE4`, muted `#A79E90`,
  line `#332C24`, accent `#F5813F`, gold `#F2B23C`, teal `#7FA6B7`.
- **Type:** serif display (Iowan Old Style / Palatino / Georgia — AR: Zarid Slab fits
  naturally), system-font body, monospace kickers/labels (uppercase, letter-spaced).
- **Shapes:** narrow reading measure, kickers with a short orange dash, two-color
  ours-vs-theirs panels, chips for lists, an ink-block "next step" panel, generous space.
- **Rule:** footer always says it's an internal working document, not a branded external one.

## Identity C — Direct Product · المنتج

**For:** software — this app, dashboards, portals, generators, tools.

- **Colors:** orange `#F47A1F`, ink `#1C1E2B`, muted `#7C8194` / `#9AA1B6`,
  hairline + wash surfaces; semantics: green `#16B364`, red `#F0453A` / `#D92D20`,
  amber `#F79009`, blue `#2E90FA`, purple `#7A5AF8`. Semantic colors are for state only —
  orange stays the single accent.
- **Type:** system fonts (SF Pro / Segoe / -apple-system); Arabic Cairo/Tajawal.
  No exotic webfonts — matches all three live Direct systems, keeps the app fast.
- **Shapes:** dense tables, Roles + Status as first-class chip columns, onboarding status
  on companies, KPI stat tiles, toasts for feedback.
- **House rule (all Direct systems):** proof on every number — every figure links to its
  source (attachment, DPIN, ID reference).

## Logo rules (all identities)

- White logo on orange/dark; slate (`#323E49`) logo on light. Vector SVG for print/scale.
- Never recolor, never stretch. Clear space ≥ the height of the "D".
- The swoosh (bird) mark can stand alone as a footer/divider motif in Classic documents.

## Which identity, in one line

> Client-facing → **Classic** · meant to be read internally → **Editorial** · software → **Product**.

The files: `brand/tokens.css` carries all three identities as CSS variables
(`data-identity="classic" | "editorial" | "product"`). `brand/identity.html` is the visual
showcase of all three with live samples, EN + AR.
