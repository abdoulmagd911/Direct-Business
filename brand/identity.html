First, the famous question

The three oranges — not a mistake

ثلاثة برتقالات — وليست خطأ

Three orange values exist in real Direct material. Each has a job. Never "fix" one to match another.

#F06820
Document orangeProposals, profiles, offers, agreements — anything printed or sent as PDF. Slightly muted, print-safe.
#FF6C00
Logo-mark orangeThe logo artwork itself, favicons, app icons, tiny marks. Sampled from the Illustrator logo master.
#F47A1F
Product orangeThe app UI. Family with the Payments hub (#fc8004) and corporate portal (#e66000).

The mark

Logo rules — same in every identity

قواعد الشعار — واحدة في كل الهويات

  • White logo on orange or dark; slate logo on light. The vector master (`direct_logo_vector.svg`) is the white logo on its orange plate — use it whenever print or scaling matters.
  • Never recolor, never stretch. Clear space around it ≥ the height of the "D".
  • The swoosh can stand alone as a footer / divider motif in Classic documents.
  • Mark orange is #FF6C00, wordmark slate #323E49 — for favicons and tiny marks, match the logo file, not the document palette.

The decision, in one table

Which identity, when

أي هوية تستخدم، ومتى

You're making…IdentityWhy
Company profile, proposal, offer, agreement, flyerA · CLASSICIt represents Direct to the outside. Official palette, Proxima Nova + Zarid Slab, the fee table.
Board readout, report, analysis, working proposalB · EDITORIALIt's meant to be read and decided on. Calm serif reading page, dark mode, internal footer.
App screen, dashboard, portal, offer generator UIC · PRODUCTIt's software. System fonts, semantic state colors, dense data patterns, proof on every number.
An offer generator's output (the PDF it produces)A · CLASSICThe tool is Product; the document it prints for a client is Classic.
